Kith Universe is an adaptable, low-sensory digital sanctuary. We deliver immediate executive support, emotional anchoring, and manageable next steps through an AI peer-support ecosystem built for neurodivergent minds.

We honor your unique cognitive bandwidth.

Most productivity apps try to "fix" executive dysfunction with streaks, gamification, and high-pressure alerts that ultimately lead to burnout.

Kith Universe is different. We don't track your vitals or shame you for dropping a task. We built a peer-support ecosystem that adapts to your sensory needs and gives you the exact level of support you can handle right now.

Emotional Anchoring

Meet Kith

When you are overwhelmed or under-stimulated, Kith is your warm, judgment-free sounding board. A safe place to braindump, untangle your thoughts, and find your center without pressure.

Immediate Executive Support

Meet Atlas

When the friction of task initiation feels impossible, Atlas provides the first step. No long lectures. Just one manageable micro-step, a timer, and the asynchronous body-doubling presence to help you move forward.

Low-Sensory Space

Meet Haven

When the digital world is too loud, our UI literally dims to protect your nervous system. Haven strips away the noise, offering literal, minimal words and an explicit off-ramp to step away without guilt.

Zero streaks. Zero shame. Zero pressure.
